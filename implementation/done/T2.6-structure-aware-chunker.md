**Depends on:** T2.2–T2.5.
**Goal:** Turn `ParsedDocument`s into self-describing chunks with the exact spec rules.

**Spec:**
- `BODY_SPLITTER`: 700 chars, 100 overlap, separators `["\n## ", "\n### ", "\n\n", "\n", ". ", " "]`.
- `SLIDE_SPLITTER`: 300 chars, 50 overlap.
- `MIN_CHUNK_CHARS = 80`.
- `DROP_SECTIONS = {"references", "bibliography", "appendix"}`.
- The abstract is one atomic chunk; its `chunk_id` is `f"{doc_id}::abstract"`.
- Section detection: normalized heading match against `abstract`, `introduction`, `related work`, `methodology`, `methods`, `results`, `discussion`, `conclusion`, `references`, `bibliography`, `appendix`; otherwise `body`.
- Breadcrumb exactly:
  `[Project: {title} | Author(s): {names} | Year: {year} | Program: {program} | Section: {section} | p.{page}]`
- Chunk text is `breadcrumb + "\n" + body`.
- Payload keys: `project_id`, `title_en`, `senior_ids`, `author_names_normalized`, `doc_id`, `doc_type`, `year`, `program`, `domain`, `tech_stack`, `supervisor_id`, `section`, `page_number`, `chunk_index`, `chunk_id`, `breadcrumb`, `is_primary_doc`.
- `is_primary_doc` is true only for `doc_type="report"`.
- Drop chunks shorter than 80 chars after breadcrumb removal? No: measure the body before adding the breadcrumb.

**Interfaces:**
