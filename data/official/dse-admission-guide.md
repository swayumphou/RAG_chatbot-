# How to apply to the DSE program at RUPP after Grade 12

How can I apply to the Data Science and Engineering (DSE) bachelor program at the Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP) after Grade 12?

Requirements to apply. Generally, you should:
1. Complete Grade 12.
2. Obtain your Cambodian Baccalaureate (Bac II).
3. Meet the admission requirements for the DSE program.
4. Complete the application process and any required entrance examination.

Important: DSE has its own admission process, so do not assume that passing Bac II automatically guarantees admission.
